# Acme Corporation - Employee Handbook

## 1. Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday,
with a one-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours, during which all
employees are expected to be available, are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per
calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Leave accrues at 2 days
per completed month of service.

A maximum of 5 unused leave days may be carried forward into the
following calendar year. Days beyond this limit are forfeited on 31
December.

Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis calculated
against their contracted hours.

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
certificate is required for any absence exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## 4. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and
are reviewed every 6 months.

## 5. Notice Period

The notice period during probation is 15 days. After confirmation, the
notice period is 60 days for all employees below manager grade, and 90
days for manager grade and above.

## 6. Reimbursement Policy

Reference: policy code HR-114. Travel and client-entertainment expenses
must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred. Claims submitted
after 30 days require director-level approval.
