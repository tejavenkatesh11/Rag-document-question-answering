ACME CORPORATION - IT SECURITY POLICY
Document reference: SEC-2024-01

1. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS

All account passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include
uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. Passwords
must be changed every 90 days. The previous 5 passwords may not be
reused.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts that can
access customer data.

2. DEVICE SECURITY

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Screen lock
must activate after 5 minutes of inactivity.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2
hours of discovery.

3. REMOTE ACCESS

Remote access to internal systems requires a connection through the
company VPN. Employees working remotely must not use public Wi-Fi to
access production systems without the VPN active.

Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week, consistent with the
Employee Handbook, but any remote access to production databases
additionally requires an approved engineering access request.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
Restricted data may only be stored on company-managed systems and may
never be copied to personal storage or personal email.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example
within 1 hour of discovery. The security team acknowledges reports
within 4 working hours.
