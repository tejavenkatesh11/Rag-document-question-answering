# Acme Corporation - Travel and Expense Policy

Policy code: FIN-220. This policy operates alongside the reimbursement
section of the Employee Handbook (HR-114).

## Submission Deadlines

All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the date the
expense was incurred. Claims submitted between 30 and 60 days require
director-level approval. Claims older than 60 days will not be
reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Travel Booking

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class. International flights
exceeding 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class
requires prior CFO approval.

Hotel accommodation is capped at 8,000 INR per night for metro cities
and 5,000 INR per night for non-metro locations.

## Daily Allowance

Employees travelling on business receive a daily meal allowance of
1,500 INR for metro cities and 1,000 INR for non-metro locations.
Receipts are not required for the daily allowance.

## Client Entertainment

Client entertainment expenses require pre-approval from the reporting
manager when the total exceeds 10,000 INR. Alcohol is reimbursable only
when part of a client entertainment claim.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved claims are reimbursed with the next payroll cycle, provided
approval is completed at least 5 working days before payroll processing.
